# Design System: Zelaya Lawn & Landscape

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Yard You Can Touch"**

The page is a fall lawn, not a website about one. The ground is deep lawn green cut into diagonal mowing stripes; grass grows along the bottom of the opening and closing bands, autumn leaves fall onto it, and a finger or cursor blows them away like a leaf blower. Sections meet at rolling turf seams rather than straight edges. Everything else stays out of the way of one action: text Zelaya for a free estimate.

The voice is the owner's flyer, promoted: heavy uppercase sans headlines that read like a yard sign, the chrome-serif ZELAYA wordmark from the logo, logo lime and chrome silver as trim, and maple gold and orange as the warm notes. The type stack is the system's own sans and serif, no web fonts; Ellie pinned this house style over the skill's display-face rule, so it is a recorded exception, not a default for other projects. Density is phone-first and generous: big tap targets, short service rows, one lime Text button repeated unchanged, and the phone number always visible in the top bar.

Confirmed rejections: black grounds (the flyer is black; the page is green), glow shadows, thin highlight rims, stock or AI photography shown as Zelaya's work, and any fact the owner has not stated.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Deep green ground in 115° mowing stripes, never black.
- Living turf: canvas grass and blowable leaves in the hero and the close; a few leaves drift down the page.
- Rolling turf seams between every section.
- Uppercase 900-weight system sans for display; chrome system serif for the wordmark and the promise line.
- One in-page action, the lime Text pill; calling lives in the top-bar number pill.
- Gold carries the offer (chip and desktop deal swatch); lime, gold and orange leaf markers name the towns; lime carries every action.

## Colors

A lawn at dusk: three steps of deep green ground, a cream clearing, logo lime and chrome for trim, and autumn leaf colors as the only warmth.

### Primary
- **Logo Leaf Lime** (lime): action and living turf. Primary button fill and border, the top-bar call pill's text and border, service-row arrow wells on hover, focus ring, selection, season heads, the "Yard ready for winter." half of the hero headline, the lockup label, footer links, the default town leaf marker, and the middle stroke of every turf seam.
- **Sunlit Lime** (lime-bright): primary button hover only.
- **Mown Lime** (lime-deep): the scrollbar thumb.
- **Promise Lime** (lime-ink): the lime that holds contrast on cream; used for the "Year-round care." clause of the promise line. Lime never sets text on cream at its logo value.

### Secondary
- **Chrome Silver** (chrome): the ZELAYA wordmark in the top bar and the close, nothing else. The Z mark itself is a chrome gradient (#f6f8f9 to #7b858b) with a lime leaf; it is the logo, not a palette. The same mark ships as favicon.svg and apple-touch-icon.png.

### Tertiary
- **Maple Gold** (leaf-gold): the offer. The 10%-off chip in the hero and the close, and the desktop deal swatch in the services grid. Text on it is **Bark Ink** (gold-ink).
- **Maple Orange** (leaf-orange): town leaf markers only.
- **Maple Red** (leaf-red): declared in the token block; no UI surface uses it now. Red lives in the canvas leaves.
- The canvas leaves use their own five radial-gradient pairs in the same family (orange, red, gold, rust, amber); they are sprite pigment, not UI color.

### Neutral
- **Lawn Green** (ground): page ground, hero and area (striped with **Mowing Stripe**, stripe), browser theme color.
- **Shade Green** (ground-2): services and close sections.
- **Night Green** (ground-3): footer, text on lime.
- **Clearing Cream** (cream): the one light section ("Your yard, in good hands").
- **Field Ink** (ink, same value as ground-2) and **Moss Ink** (ink-soft): headline and care text on cream.
- **Frost** (text) and **Sage** (text-soft): headline and supporting text on green; Sage also tints the resting service-row arrow.
- Hairline dividers between service rows are Sage at 18% alpha (1px). Resting arrow wells are Frost at 8% alpha.

### Named Rules
**The Green Ground Rule.** The darkest surface is Night Green. No section, bar, overlay or card is black or neutral grey; any translucent layer is green-tinted.

**The Lime Means Act Rule.** Lime is the color of action and the living turf: the button, the call pill, focus, seams, season heads, the headline's second clause. Resting arrow wells stay neutral and turn lime only under the pointer. Warm leaf colors never sit on a button, and no button is any color but lime.

**The Gold Is the Offer Rule.** Maple Gold with Bark Ink text marks the 10%-off offer, wherever it appears. Gold, orange and lime otherwise appear only as small leaf markers, never as fills.

## Typography

**Display Font:** System sans (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial)
**Body Font:** Same system sans
**Wordmark Font:** System serif (Iowan Old Style, Palatino Linotype, Palatino, Book Antiqua, Georgia)

**Character:** A yard sign and a brass nameplate. The sans shouts in heavy uppercase like the flyer's "FALL CLEANUPS NOW BOOKING"; the serif is reserved for the name and the owner's own promise, set wide and chrome. No web fonts: the page loads on cellular in a Facebook in-app browser, and the system stack is the house style Ellie pinned for this project.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (900, clamp 2.35rem to 5.6rem, line-height 0.95, uppercase, -0.02em, balanced wrap): the hero headline only. Its second clause turns lime.
- **Headline** (900, clamp 2rem to 4rem, 0.95, uppercase): section heads. The close's headline is the smaller headline-close step (max 18ch) because it sits under the wordmark.
- **Deal** (900, 2.4rem, uppercase): the desktop deal swatch, with an 800 0.95rem +0.06em subline.
- **Town** (800, clamp 1.5rem to 2.1rem, 1.15, -0.01em, sentence case): the service-area list; big enough to read as the answer to "do you come to me?".
- **Title** (800, 1.18rem, 1.15, uppercase): service row names.
- **Season** (900, 1.05rem, uppercase, +0.06em, lime): season group heads, paired with a 30px line icon.
- **Care** (400, clamp 1.125rem to 1.375rem, 1.5, max 38ch): the care paragraph on cream, one step above body.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.55): running text, Sage on green; measure 36 to 46ch. The "and the surrounding areas" line is body at 500 in Sage.
- **Small text:** footer at 0.95rem; service sublines at 0.9rem; season notes ("booking now", "next up") at 0.875rem, all 400 in Sage.
- **Small label** (800, 0.95rem): the offer chip and the call pill (1rem on desktop, 0.9rem under 375px).
- **Button label** (800, 1.0625rem, +0.01em, sentence case).
- **Wordmark** (serif 700, +0.08em, uppercase, chrome): ZELAYA at 1.3rem in the top bar (1.6rem desktop, 1.15rem under 375px), clamp 2.3rem to 4.5rem in the close.
- **Serif statement** (serif 700, clamp 1.25rem to 1.75rem, 1.25, max 28ch; clamp 1.6rem to 2.4rem on desktop): the owner's promise line on cream.
- **Lockup label** (sans, uppercase, lime): "LAWN & LANDSCAPE" under the wordmark only, as part of the logo lockup; 600 0.7rem +0.2em in the top bar (+0.1em under 430px), 700 0.8rem +0.3em in the close.

### Named Rules
**The Shout and Sign Rule.** Headings and service names are uppercase 900/800 sans; sentences are sentence case 400. Nothing else is uppercase except the logo lockup and the deal swatch.

**The Serif Is the Name Rule.** The serif appears only for the ZELAYA wordmark and the owner's own promise line. It never sets a heading, button or body text.

## Layout

Phone-first single column at a 16px gutter, widening to a 40px gutter and a 1200px max container at 760px (the main breakpoint; scripts treat ≤759px as phone and scale every motion constant down). Two narrow-phone steps tighten the top bar: under 430px the call pill drops its icon and the lockup tracking narrows; under 375px the mark, wordmark and pill shrink. Rhythm on phones is about 28 to 44px above a section and 80 to 84px below; on desktop 48 to 72px above and 120px below. The hero and the close add a 150px (200px desktop) bottom band for the grass to live in.

- **First phone screen:** logo bar with the number pill, display headline, gold offer chip, one full-width lime Text button, with grass and leaves underneath.
- **Desktop:** the hero splits 1.35fr / 0.65fr with the large Z mark on the right; the button sits at its natural width; services become two columns (Fall and Winter plus the gold deal swatch on the left, the lawn-and-landscape list spanning the right); the cream section puts the heading full width over the care paragraph and the promise line in two columns (1.1fr / 0.9fr, bottom-aligned); area splits heading and town list 1fr / 1fr, with the towns in two auto columns 48px apart.
- **Close:** centered stack of Z mark (112px), wordmark, lockup, headline, urge line, Text button, offer chip.

**The Thumb Rule.** Every tap target is at least 44px; buttons are 58px tall on phones (62px desktop), the call pill 44px (46px desktop), and service rows 64px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is physical, not interface chrome: grass blades drawn back to front, leaves resting in front of or behind blades by depth, and turf seams overlapping the section below. Shadows are few, dark, downward, and spread-negative so they read as something sitting on the lawn.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Button lift** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -8px rgba(4,18,9,.6)`): primary button only.
- **Chip set-down** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 18px -8px rgba(0,0,0,.5)`): the tilted offer chip.
- **Swatch set-down** (`box-shadow: 0 14px 30px -14px rgba(4,18,9,.7)`): the desktop deal swatch.
- **Mark drop** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 22px 30px rgba(5,20,10,.55))`): the desktop hero Z mark.

### Named Rules
**The Lying-on-Grass Rule.** A shadow is always dark, offset away from the light, and tighter than its blur (negative spread). No colored glows, no zero-offset halos, no inner highlights.

## Shapes

Two families. Controls are fully round: the pill button, the pill call control, and circular arrow wells. Promotional pieces are hand-cut: the offer chip (6px 14px 8px 16px, tilted -1.5°) and the deal swatch (10px 26px 8px 30px, tilted -2°) have unequal corners and a slight rotation, like a tag stapled to a flyer. Town markers are small leaf silhouettes (0.62em) masked from the same leaf path as the line icon. Section edges are never straight; they are the rolling turf seams. Focus rings are a 3px lime outline with a 3px offset and 10px corners.

**The Cut Tag Rule.** Only the offer and the deal get asymmetric corners and a tilt. Everything you tap is perfectly round.

## Components

### Buttons
One button, big, round and identical everywhere it appears.
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), 2px lime border.
- **Primary (Text for a free estimate):** lime fill, Night Green label with a message line icon, button-lift shadow. Opens SMS with a pre-filled message. Appears once in the hero and once in the close.
- **Hover (hover-capable devices only):** lightens to Sunlit Lime and rises 2px. Press scales to 0.97. Transform eases on cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) over 0.25s.
- **Layout:** full width on phones, natural width from 760px (left in the hero, centered in the close).

### Call Pill (top bar)
The only call control on the page, top right in the hero.
- 2px lime border at 50% alpha, transparent fill, lime 800 label showing the number at every width, with a phone icon from 430px up.
- 44px tall, 0 14px padding (46px tall, 0 18px, 1rem label on desktop; 0 12px and 0.9rem under 375px).
- **Hover:** 14% lime wash and a full-lime border.

### Service Rows
A list, not cards: each service is one tappable row that texts Zelaya with that service filled in.
- Uppercase 800 title, optional Sage subline, 1px Sage-18% hairline above each row and below the last.
- Right-aligned 40px circular arrow well, neutral at rest (Frost 8% wash, Sage arrow). On hover it fills lime with a Night Green arrow and slides 4px right; on press it scales to 0.9.

### Season Heads
A 30px lime line icon (leaf, snowflake, sprout), the season name in the Season style, and an optional right-aligned Sage note ("booking now", "next up"). Groups are Fall, Winter, and Lawn, beds & landscape.

### Offer Chip and Deal Swatch
- **Offer chip:** inline Maple Gold tag with a tag icon, small label in Bark Ink, cut-tag corners, -1.5° tilt, chip set-down shadow. Appears in the hero and the close.
- **Deal swatch (desktop only):** Maple Gold block with Bark Ink type in the services grid, deal type with a subline, cut-tag corners, -2° tilt, swatch set-down shadow.

### Town List
A plain list, no fill, border or pill: each town in the Town style led by a 0.62em leaf marker. Markers cycle lime, Maple Gold on even items, Maple Orange on every third. The trailing "and the surrounding areas" line drops the marker and steps down to Sage body at 500.

### Care and Promise (cream section)
The cream clearing holds a Moss Ink care paragraph in the Care style and the owner's promise in the serif statement, its last clause in Promise Lime. Stacked on phones; two columns under a full-width heading on desktop.

### Top Bar
Z mark (44px, 54px desktop; 38px under 375px) beside the stacked chrome-serif wordmark and lime lockup label; the call pill on the right.

### Living Turf (signature)
Canvas grass and leaves in the hero and the close; the motion is the brand.
- **Grass:** blades as filled tapered quadratic curves in eight greens (#163f22 to #86cc52), sorted back to front, with a soil gradient underneath. They sway on a shared three-sine wind and part around the pointer on a spring (stiffness 38, damping 6.5) with a wobble back.
- **Leaves:** maple and ovate sprites, flipping and spinning, settling into the grass at their depth. The pointer acts as a leaf blower; a gust band sweeps left to right every 6 to 10 seconds and lifts resting leaves.
- **Tunable constants (phone / desktop):** blade density 0.95 / 0.85 per px, grass band 118 / 172px, leaves 30 / 54, leaf size 18-32 / 20-40px, blow radius 110 / 150px, blow strength 2600, velocity carry 0.55, grass parting radius 70 / 90px. These live as named constants at the top of the turf function and are the intended tuning surface.
- **Page drift:** a fixed full-viewport canvas with 5 / 11 leaves (14-22 / 16-28px) drifting below the hero, also blowable (radius 100 / 130px).
- **Turf seams:** 56px (80px desktop) SVG bands on an 80-unit viewBox between sections. Three rolling stroked lines (back line in mid-green or a straw tone under cream, a 3.5px lime middle line, a front line in mid-green or pale straw on cream) over a fill in the next section's ground that follows the middle line. Lines roll with two swells and a ripple; point step 20, speed 1.0.
- **Performance:** one shared requestAnimationFrame loop; each canvas or seam set pauses when off screen; device pixel ratio capped at 2.
- **Reduced motion:** grass and leaves draw once, still; page drift is off; seams freeze at their first frame; button and arrow transitions are removed; smooth scroll is off. A static CSS grass gradient covers the hero before scripts run.

### Share and Icon Assets
favicon.svg (the chrome Z mark with its lime leaf, heavier stroke for small sizes), apple-touch-icon.png (180px, from the same mark) and og.jpg (1200 x 630) carry the identity outside the page. New share or icon assets derive from the Z mark on the green ground.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep every ground green (Night Green is the floor) and use the 115° mowing stripe (64px bands of ground and stripe) on the hero and the area band.
- **Do** offer contact as the one lime Text pill in the hero and the close, with the number always visible in the top-bar call pill.
- **Do** make every service a row that texts Zelaya with the service pre-filled, with a neutral arrow well that turns lime on hover.
- **Do** mark the offer in Maple Gold with Bark Ink, as the cut-tag chip or the desktop swatch.
- **Do** join sections with rolling turf seams whose fill matches the next section.
- **Do** tune motion through the named turf and drift constants, scaling them down on phones, and keep the reduced-motion still frame.
- **Do** keep tap targets at 44px or more, and buttons at 58 to 62px tall.
- **Do** use the system sans and serif stacks on this project; no web fonts.
- **Do** set lime text on cream in Promise Lime, never the logo lime.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use a black or neutral-grey ground anywhere, including bars and overlays.
- **Don't** use glow shadows: no colored blur, no lime halo, no zero-offset glow. Shadows are dark, downward and spread-negative.
- **Don't** add thin highlight lines: no 1px light rims, inner top highlights or glassy borders on buttons, chips or panels. The turf seam strokes and the faint row hairlines are the only hairlines.
- **Don't** state facts the owner hasn't given: no review stars, testimonials, rating badges, years-in-business ribbons, prices, hours or owner name, and no stock or AI-generated photos presented as Zelaya's work.
- **Don't** put warm leaf colors on buttons, or use gold for anything but the offer and town markers.
- **Don't** set headings in the serif or buttons in uppercase.
- **Don't** turn services into a card grid or towns into chips; both are plain lists.
